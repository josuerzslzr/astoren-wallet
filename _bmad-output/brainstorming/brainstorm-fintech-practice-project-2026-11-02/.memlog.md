- (insight by user) Saver (Marta) expects roughly APR/12 credited monthly; seeing $0 returns after a month, or a balance decrease with no orders placed, reads as fishy -> would withdraw
- (question by coach) Open risk: if pool funds back house quotes and FX moves against the house, who absorbs the loss - savers' principal or the house? Directly drives Marta's trust
- (insight by user) House holds both USD and EUR, so a currency move is only a real (realized) loss when it sells a currency below what it paid - asked how exchanges normally handle this
- (insight by coach) Real-world models: (1) market makers/FX brokers hedge - inventory limits, skew quotes to rebalance, offload to interbank; spread pays for the risk; (2) DeFi AMM pools - LPs absorb 'impermanent loss', variable yield; (3) Wise/Revolut-style - company treasury bears FX risk, savers paid from money-market interest, principal protected (regulated). Insurance/reserve fund funded by spread absorbs losses first
- (idea by coach) MVP recommendation: per-currency pools + house bears FX risk; savers get fixed APR in their deposit currency accrued daily; house protects itself with exposure limits and quote skewing; a reserve fund fed by a slice of the spread
- (decision by user) Defer APR/savings-yield (savers funding house pool) to a later phase; keep logged ideas but exclude from MVP
- (insight by coach) MVP impact of deferring yield: house liquidity = platform-owned seeded treasury balances (simulated); no saver pool, accrual jobs, interest ledger entries or reserve fund in MVP; house keeps exposure limits + widen/pause quote; balances can still be held in-wallet, just without interest; Marta/LP streak rewards move to later phases
- (question by user) How could users take advantage? Should we allow it - FX-savvy users profiting a bit could be a reason to prefer the wallet; don't market it as trading, but should we restrict it?
- (idea by coach) Healthy trading (allow): P2P market makers posting tight pegged orders and earning the spread; arbitrage between our P2P board and the house rate - these traders ARE the liquidity
- (idea by coach) Abuse: stale-rate sniping - house quote lags the market feed, bots take it before it updates; guard with quote TTL/quote-lock (price valid N seconds, re-validate at take) and max staleness check
- (idea by coach) Abuse: round-trip arbitrage against the house (buy EUR from house, sell back to house at a profit when the feed jumps); guard with spread > typical tick move, exposure limits, per-user velocity limits
- (idea by coach) Abuse: concurrency exploits - double-take, double-withdraw, spending held funds; guard with conditional updates, holds, idempotency keys, CHECK constraints
- (idea by coach) Abuse: webhook forgery/replay - fake or replayed top-up webhooks crediting money; guard with HMAC signatures, timestamp window, event-id dedupe
- (idea by coach) Abuse: withdraw before top-up settles (pending vs available funds); guard with pending balance state until gateway confirms
- (idea by coach) Abuse: spoofing / order spam (post and cancel many orders to move the board), self-matching / wash trades between own accounts; guard with order rate limits, min order size, no self-match
- (idea by coach) Abuse: rounding exploits - many micro-trades harvesting rounding in the user's favour; guard with integer minor units / decimal math, banker's rounding toward house, min trade size
- (insight by coach) Recommendation: allow and welcome P2P trading/market making (it is the liquidity), don't market as a trading platform; protect the house and integrity with the guards above
- (decision by user) Keep ALL abuse guards in MVP scope
- (question by user) Should guards be separate stories, an abuse epic, or acceptance criteria on feature stories?
- (insight by coach) Recommend: guards as ACs on the feature they protect (take-order owns double-take/idempotency; top-up owns HMAC/replay/pending balance; ledger owns minor units/rounding/CHECK; house quote owns quote-lock/staleness/exposure); only cross-cutting ones as own stories (rate limiting + order spam, self-match prevention); avoid a trailing 'abuse epic' that gets cut and leaves features unsafe.
